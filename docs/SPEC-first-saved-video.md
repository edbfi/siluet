# First saved video

**Approved for implementation.** This is the first slice of Video Studio, Jobs and Gallery in the [capability map](SPEC.md). One signed-in creator uses their ToAPIs key to review and queue one Seedance 2.5 text-to-video request with zero retries. They follow it in Jobs while Siluet saves it automatically, then play, seek, download or remove the saved clip after restarting with the provider offline. This slice builds on the [first saved image](SPEC-first-saved-image.md) and [D1–D5](decisions.md). Provider references were checked on 2026-11-22.

## Submit and track

Send one Bearer-authenticated JSON `POST https://toapis.com/v1/videos/generations`. The outbound body contains only these fields. The narrower duration, resolution and prompt limits are Siluet release choices.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `model` | `"seedance-2-5"` |
| `video_operation` | `"generate"` |
| `prompt` | Nonblank trimmed text, at most 4,000 UTF-8 bytes |
| `duration` | Integer `4`–`10`, in seconds |
| `aspect_ratio` | `"16:9"`, `"9:16"`, `"1:1"`, `"4:3"`, `"3:4"` or `"21:9"` |
| `resolution` | `"480p"` or `"720p"` |
| `generate_audio` | Selected JSON boolean |
| `output_format` | `"mp4"` |
| `return_last_frame` | `false` |
| `disable_nsfw` | Top-level JSON boolean `false`, fixed by D4 |
| `client_business_id` | Application-generated `siluet-` plus a UUIDv4, durably reserved before dispatch |

The model schema documents the D4 field. Account and region behavior comes from D4's ToAPIs policy. The model docs do not establish the exact fallback route, its price, or how to discover eligibility through the model key. Provider review or rejection can still occur. Block known incompatible routes before Queue and preserve any policy rejection without changing the field or sending again. Use the generation endpoint's published options over the broader marketing guide. No `n`, reference URLs, upload, draft mode, last-frame asset, tools, callback, or client-supplied routing fields are needed. ## Save and play offline

On completion, Siluet saves `result.type: "video"` with one `result.data` item automatically, as for images ([D9](decisions.md#d9--save-automatically)). Use `url` first, then at most one optional `fallback_url` for that item if the primary is inaccessible over an otherwise permitted route. This delivery fallback is a download of the existing task, distinct from generation routing. A policy violation or failed media verification stops Save. Show returned `expires_at` (Unix seconds) when valid and skip media transfer if the latest status says the result has expired. ToAPIs documents 30-day result availability, and original links may fail sooner. Show missing expiry as unknown. Retry download can refresh status for the same task within its read limits. A missing or inaccessible link alone leaves expiry unknown. Link renewal and retention of upstream copies are unverified. Distinguish confirmed expiry, other download failures and unsupported results from generation failure. [Video results and expiration](https://docs.toapis.com/docs/en/api-reference/tasks/video-status#resource-expiration) · [Retention policy](https://toapis.com/data-retention)

Extend Gallery's existing private Save, durability, single-result publication and removal safeguards. Save streams the full HTTPS result to generated-name private scratch. Apply the existing public-address and redirect checks to every hop of either URL, with no API credentials or cookies at the media host. Enforce a 64 MiB encoded cap, a 60-second total transfer budget including redirects and fallback, and one video Save at a time in the single runtime. With a fallback URL reserve up to 30 seconds for the primary. Failure to resolve, connect or obtain a successful response on an authorized primary permits fallback within the total budget. A destination-policy or redirect violation, oversized body, non-identity or partial response, mid-body truncation or length mismatch, or failed verification ends Save for this attempt. A result has at most one save at a time. A save of another clip waits for the active one. Refuse partial or non-identity-encoded responses, verify any declared length against the received body, and discard partial files after failure. Startup removes leftover partial files. Keep result URLs server-side and transient. [Image Save safeguards](SPEC-first-saved-image.md#save-reopen-and-remove)

Verify the stored file's structure in TypeScript under [D6](decisions.md#d6--verify-downloaded-video-structure-in-typescript), with no native tools, independently of filename, HTTP MIME and the provider's `format` hint. Require a self-contained, unencrypted, non-fragmented MP4 with one H.264 (`avc1`) video track and at most one AAC-LC (`mp4a`) mono or stereo track at up to 48 kHz. Every SPS must be Baseline, Main or High, 8-bit 4:2:0, with a cropped size equal to the sample entry, at most 1,920 pixels per edge and 2,073,600 pixels in area. Parse the entire top-level box sequence, buffering only `ftyp` and `moov` (at most 4 MiB). Refuse unparsed trailing bytes, external references, extra tracks, unsupported boxes and HDR colour descriptions. One C2PA manifest box after the file type box and before the movie and its media data is allowed and kept, per D6. Map every sample into media data without overlap. Each track's decode duration must equal its header, apart from the AAC priming case D6 allows, its edit list must start inside its composition timeline, end within it and select at least one sample, and the presentation must last more than zero and at most 12 seconds. A file that ends inside a box counts as a failed download. A WebM or Matroska file is refused with its own message. These checks cover the declared configuration; the browser decodes the samples when it plays. BytePlus describes 8-bit 480p and 720p and HEVC at 1080p, while the ToAPIs generation docs specify MP4 without guaranteeing its codecs on every route. The local limits are Siluet's acceptance profile. Surface an unsupported result without transcoding or a new generation. [BytePlus output notes](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ModelArk/seedance-2-5)

Publish the verified original bytes and one Gallery entry atomically from the owner's perspective. Save replay converges on the same entry. Record media type, byte length, verified dimensions and presented duration in addition to existing job metadata, accepted quote and available billing. Extend the existing Gallery card and detail viewer with a video type label and duration. A video tile with a play glyph is sufficient. The detail view uses the saved clip without fetching or producing a separate thumbnail. Confirm removal of the local entry and file, recover interrupted removal on restart, and retain the job outcome. Keep prompts, keys, URLs, paths, raw responses and verification details out of durable job metadata, browser storage and logs. [Image Gallery lifecycle](SPEC-first-saved-image.md#save-reopen-and-remove)

Serve video only from the authenticated local Gallery media route, including for `HEAD`, Range and attachment requests. Reject requests with an explicitly foreign `Origin` or a present `Sec-Fetch-Site` other than `same-origin` or `none`. Stream the verified regular file with `video/mp4`, `private, no-store`, `nosniff`, same-origin resource policy and a generated download name. Implement `Accept-Ranges: bytes`, full `200` responses and bounded single-range `206` responses with exact `Content-Range` and `Content-Length`. Support explicit, open-ended and suffix byte ranges. Return `416` with `Content-Range: bytes */<size>` for an unsatisfiable supported range. Ignore malformed, unsupported-unit or multi-range headers and send the full representation. Apply Range to `GET` only, using a full response when `If-Range` cannot be validated. Stream from one checked regular-file handle, verifying identity and size against the saved record. HEAD reads no media payload; a valid open-ended range streams through EOF without buffering the whole file. A missing file is unavailable. Playback and seeking work after restart and credential removal with zero provider traffic. [HTTP range semantics](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110.html#section-14)

Before each business-ID lookup, atomically reserve the read against the local job, stored business ID and matching credential revision. Persist its cooldown across tabs and restarts, including while the provider task ID is unknown. Install a matching task ID once, conditional on the job still having none. A late acknowledgement naming the same task converges on the same job. A conflicting identity preserves the verified association and reports an uncertain lookup. Ordinary status reads require a successful generation.task envelope with the stored task ID, expected model, and matching business ID when present before applying state, billing, expiry or result URLs. Reject a completed response with the right task ID and a contradictory model. Status examples use different placeholders in the request and response. Real task-ID equivalence and model aliases remain unverified. On mismatch preserve the last known state, withhold media from Save and point the owner to provider history.

## Coverage and follow-ups

- ToAPIs `seedance-2-5` ordinary text-to-video is documented and pending until the built-app checks pass. Longer and automatic durations, adaptive framing, 1080p, MOV, draft/final, keyframes, edits, extension and multimodal references are documented and pending. The marketing guide mentions 4K, while the production endpoint schema lists resolutions through 1080p. Leave 4K route support unknown. Poyo.ai `seedance-2.5` text-to-video is documented. Its published input schema omits the D4 field and rejects extra properties, so the route stays unavailable under D4. The video page explains why a Poyo-only owner cannot Queue this model, while the offline ToAPIs composer and Review remain accessible. [ToAPIs contract](https://docs.toapis.com/docs/en/api-reference/videos/seedance-2-5/generation) · [Poyo.ai contract](https://docs.poyo.ai/api-manual/video-series/seedance-2.5)
- Later specs can reuse Review and Jobs for explicit `first_frame` and `last_frame` image roles, `reference_image`, `reference_video` and `reference_audio` combinations, and `video_operation: "edit"` with a video role, `duration: -1` and `aspect_ratio: "adaptive"`. Qualify the combinations and limits per operation, including asset-review waiting and rejection. Public URL review must disclose who receives the complete URL. Local inputs follow the [clean-copy proposal](SPEC-provider-media-cleanup.md) after format/route qualification, binding verified bytes, order, roles, access and retention terms before transfer. ToAPIs video upload documents MP4, WebM and MOV up to 50 MB and returns a reusable public URL. MKV is outside that route's documented formats, and WebM upload does not establish Seedance edit support. The stricter of the cleaner, upload and operation limits applies. [Video upload](https://docs.toapis.com/docs/en/api-reference/uploads/videos) · [Reference operations](https://docs.toapis.com/docs/en/api-reference/videos/seedance-2-5/generation#reference-input-limits)
